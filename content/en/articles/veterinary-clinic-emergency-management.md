Emergency veterinary cases are one of the most stressful and critical situations in clinic management. We address emergency management and on-call system strategies for veterinary clinics in 2026.

## Emergency Preparedness

Keeping emergency equipment, medication stock and qualified staff at the clinic is critical. At the same time, clearly communicating emergency procedures to pet owners manages expectations.

## On-Call and Call Management System

An on-call system should be established for night and weekend emergencies. Digital call management facilitates routing emergency cases and patient admission process.

## Pet Owner Communication

In an emergency, informing the pet owner is critical for managing anxiety and building trust. Fast, transparent and empathetic communication prevents potential complaints.
