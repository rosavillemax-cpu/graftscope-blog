Patient loyalty in an eye clinic is critical for long-term clinic success. Cataract and LASIK patients typically return for follow-up or new treatment needs. Effective CRM strategies are needed to capitalize on this potential.

## Patient Lifetime Value

An eye clinic patient goes through multiple treatment stages throughout their life: examination, treatment, follow-up, new treatment. Patient experience at each stage determines repeat preference and referral likelihood.

## Automated Patient Follow-Up

Regular post-surgery check-ups, medication reminders and next treatment recommendations increase patient loyalty. Manual tracking is both costly and error-prone.

**ClinixGlow** maximizes patient lifetime value for eye clinics with automatic patient follow-up, reminder and patient segmentation features.

## Conclusion

Patient loyalty is a critical success factor for both patient satisfaction and clinic revenue in eye clinics. Digital CRM tools make this process scalable.
