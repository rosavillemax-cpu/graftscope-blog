## What is a Marketing Pipeline?

A pipeline is a system where the stages a patient goes through from their first contact with the clinic are visually tracked. ClinixGlow's pipeline system brings **two separate paths** together:

### 1. Marketing Lead Pipeline
Managed by the marketing department. This path tracks potential patients who first contact the clinic through organic or paid ads.

**Stages:**
- **New Enquiry** — Lead's first entry into the system
- **Trying to Contact** — Team is attempting to reach the lead
- **Contacted** — Successful communication established
- **Converted** — Patient approved for appointment ✓
- **Lost** — Lead did not respond or declined ✗

### 2. Direct Appointment Pipeline
Managed by the clinic system. Serves as a shortcut for patients who request appointments directly.

**Stages:**
- **Create New Appointment** — Appointment request
- **Scheduled** — The convergence point for both pipelines
- **Completed** — Appointment took place ✓
- **Cancelled** — Appointment was cancelled ✗

## Advantages of the ClinixGlow Pipeline System

### Visibility
See at a glance which stage every patient prospect is at. Who has been contacted, who is waiting, who has been converted — track instantly.

### Automatic Tracking
When leads enter the system, they are automatically assigned to the appropriate stage. Manual entry errors and forgotten lead risks are eliminated.

### Cross-Department Coordination
When the marketing team moves a lead to the "Converted" stage, the clinic system is automatically notified. A lead in the "Converted" stage goes directly to "Scheduled."

Which channel brings more leads, which stage loses leads, conversion rates — all metrics are visible on the dashboard.

### Scalability
As your team grows, the pipeline grows. Adding new channels, defining new stages, or changing existing processes takes minutes.
