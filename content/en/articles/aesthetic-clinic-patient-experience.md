As competition in the aesthetic clinic market increases, patient experience has become the key differentiator. What do patients expect in 2026 and how can clinics meet these expectations? In this article, we examine patient experience strategies in detail.

## Stages of Patient Experience

Patient experience is shaped at every touchpoint, from the first phone call to post-treatment follow-up. Website, social media, phone, face-to-face consultation, treatment process and follow-up — each stage affects the patient's perception of the clinic.

## Digital Patient Journey

Modern patients do most of their research online. Clinic website, Instagram content and Google reviews create the first impression. Fast response, transparent pricing and detailed information are factors that increase conversion.
