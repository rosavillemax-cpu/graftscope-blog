Turkey is a significant destination attracting patients from the Middle East and Europe for eye surgery tourism. How are Turkey's competitive advantages and market dynamics shaping up in LASIK, cataract and retina treatments in 2026? This article presents strategic analysis for ophthalmology clinic operators.

## Popular Treatments and Prices

LASIK: 800-1,500 Euro (per eye), cataract: 1,000-2,500 Euro, retina treatments: 1,500-4,000 Euro. Prices in Turkey are 50-70% more affordable compared to Europe, providing a significant competitive advantage.

## Patient Profile and Expectations

Patients coming to Turkey for eye treatment demand high accuracy rates, experienced surgeons and modern equipment. Cataract patients in particular are older and travel with companions — accommodation and transfer support is critical.

## Digital Presence and Patient Trust

Doctor profiles, success rates and patient reviews on the eye clinic website are fundamental elements for building trust. Comprehensive pre-information and transparent pricing increases conversion.
